### Devin MacArthur

I build the systems between an organization's people and its data: daily pipelines, CRM integrations, AI document extraction, and the internal tools staff open every morning.

By day I run the data platform and automation for a 41,000-member statewide organization. On my own time I build agentic tooling on Claude Code, local AI pipelines, and synthesizers. Before that: eviction and transit research at Portland State University, and teaching GIS.
